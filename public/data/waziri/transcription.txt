Cha za bahar market ta rowowta ma subzai ma karala biya achanak ma jeep kai ma los wowlalai  paisa nashta movel da cha da sa wash wal cha biya za wapas lora pada garmai sha cha wama katala kani kiza pa road banda laywadal yai wh movel cha da waksa as biya ma rokhasta biya lora dukan ta subzai ma rokasta,Esa hwa me bhir market aya sabzi leny k keliye sabziya le rha tha phr achank jaib py hath mara tu paisy nhi ty mene kaha ye kia hogya phr me waps gya is garmi me jb deky tu road py phry ty phr mene kaha ye deko phr utaye or dukan gya sabzi leney

It happened like this: I went out to the market to buy vegetables. I was in the middle of buying them when I suddenly reached for my pocket and realized the money wasn't there. I said, 'What just happened?' Then I went back in this heat. When I looked, the money was lying on the road. I said, 'Look at that!' I picked it up and went to the shop to buy the vegetables.
